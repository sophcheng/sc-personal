AILURO, 2025

Educational app on AI’s impact on the planet, inspired by ailurophobia.

Alien cat-care planet minigame represents the effect of (generative) AI on our literal and cultural climate.

Selected as one of 10 winners for Deloitte x Kode With Klossy: AI & Sustainability Challenge nationwide. Awarded $5000 scholarship.

~

Made with SwiftData/SwiftUI/Xcode, original art via Procreate.

AI-NSTEIN, 2024

Informational chatbot named "Al-bot AI-nstein", introducing beginners, skeptics, etc. to AI. Created with two peers at Kode With Klossy: 2024 AI & ML Camp.

Data sourced from personal/class notes, public Kaggle datasets, & ChatGPT answers.

Worked on dataset creation & collection, UI design, wireframing, model training, and final presentation of product.
